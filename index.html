ARCHIVE INTEGRITY // 25%

There is no confirmed point at which the first Complex appeared. Some records suggest Complexes were discovered. Others suggest they were always present, waiting for someone capable of recognizing them.

A Complex is not simply an ability. It is an expression. Something psychological given form, behavior and consequence. The same power does not necessarily create the same person.

The Abyss appears to exist beneath the known world. That description may be inaccurate. Witnesses repeatedly describe the sensation of descending even when no physical movement occurs. Further records are corrupted.
